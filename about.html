ABOUT US

Who We Are

The Himalayan Local Tourism Organisation (THLTO) is the official destination marketing and tourism development entity for the Himalayan local communities.

We are committed to promoting our region as a preferred destination, fostering sustainable tourism growth, supporting local businesses and preserving our rich cultural heritage.

Together, we are building a vibrant tourism economy that creates opportunities and improves lives.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable tourism, for the economic development and welfare of local communities growth.

Our Vision

To be a leading tourism destination recognised for excellence, heritage and innovation.

Our Values

Integrity, Collaboration, Respect, Excellence, Sustainability and Inclusivity.

Our Objectives

  • ✓ Market Themselves Hike as a top tourism destination
  • ✓ Develop and support sustainable tourism products
  • ✓ Empower local communities and tourism SMMEs
  • ✓ Preserve and promote our cultural and natural heritage
  • ✓ Foster partnerships and investment in tourism
