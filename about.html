ABOUT US

Who We Are

Thembisile Hani Local Tourism is dedicated to promoting sustainable tourism and supporting the growth of local communities across Thembisile Hani, South Africa.

We work to position Thembisile Hani as an attractive tourism destination while creating meaningful opportunities for local businesses, empowering communities, celebrating cultural heritage, and protecting the natural environment.

Together, we are building a vibrant and inclusive tourism economy that creates opportunities, strengthens communities, preserves our heritage, and contributes to a better future for all.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable tourism, support economic development and improve the welfare of local communities.

Our Vision

To become a leading tourism destination recognised for excellence, heritage, sustainability and innovation.

Our Values

Integrity, collaboration, respect, excellence, sustainability and inclusivity.

Our Objectives

  • Market Thembisile Hani as a leading tourism destination.
  • Develop and support sustainable tourism products and experiences.
  • Empower local communities and tourism SMEs through tourism opportunities.
  • Preserve and promote our cultural and natural heritage.
  • Foster strategic partnerships and investment in tourism.

Tourism for Today, Heritage for Tomorrow.
